Privacy Policy

How information submitted to Marell Docs is collected and used.

Information we collect

We may collect names, contact details, document instructions, files, payment confirmation, and other information you choose to provide through our website, intake forms, or communications.

How we use information

Information is used to review requests, prepare and deliver documents, process payments, communicate about projects, improve service, and maintain business records.

Service providers

We may use third-party services for forms, payments, email, file storage, website hosting, and related operations. Those providers process information under their own privacy and security practices.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information when needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or complete a transaction requested by you.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect submitted information, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Information may be retained as reasonably needed for the purposes described above.

Your choices

Do not submit information you are not comfortable sharing through online systems. You may contact Marell Docs through the quote form with a privacy question or request.

Updates

This policy may be updated periodically. The current version will remain posted on this page.
