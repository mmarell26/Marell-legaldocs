Terms of Service

The basic terms that apply to Marell Docs projects.

Service scope

Marell Docs provides administrative document creation, organization, editing, proofreading, formatting, conversion, and related support based on customer-provided facts and instructions. The specific deliverables are stated in the accepted quote.

Not a law firm

Marell Docs is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, legal strategy, or representation. No attorney-client relationship is created.

Customer responsibility

You are responsible for providing accurate, complete, authorized information and for reviewing the draft and final files. You remain responsible for how and where a document is used.

Pricing and payment

Projects under $250 are paid in full before work begins. Projects of $250 or more normally require a 50% deposit. Work begins after payment or deposit and complete intake information are received.

Revisions and added scope

One standard revision is included unless the quote says otherwise. New facts, a changed direction, or additional deliverables may require a revised price and schedule.

Turnaround

Quoted turnaround begins after required payment and complete information are received. Rush service is subject to availability and additional pricing.

No guaranteed outcome

Marell Docs promises the defined document service, not a business, employment, financial, property, administrative, or other outcome resulting from the document’s use.

Updates

These terms may be updated periodically. Project-specific quotes or written agreements control if they contain different terms.
