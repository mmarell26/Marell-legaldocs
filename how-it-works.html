A finished service

From rough information to a ready-to-use document

You do not need perfect wording or organized files. Start with the document problem and the information you already have.

Step 1

Inquiry

Tell us what type of document you need, who will use it, what it must accomplish, and your deadline.

Step 2

Qualification

We confirm that the work is within scope and identify the exact finished result you need.

Step 3

Quote

You receive the deliverables, price, turnaround, revision limit, and information we still need.

Step 4

Payment & intake

Payment or the required deposit reserves the work. Upload your notes, files, screenshots, brand details, and required facts.

Step 5

Creation

Marell Docs organizes, drafts, edits, and formats the document with its intended reader and purpose in mind.

Step 6

Review & delivery

You review one consolidated draft, send one round of changes, and receive approved Word and/or PDF files.

What to send

Whatever you already have

We can start with incomplete or unorganized information and tell you what is missing.

  • Rough notes or a written description
  • Screenshots, photos, or text messages
  • Emails or voice-note transcripts
  • Old Word or PDF files
  • Names, dates, amounts, addresses, and facts
  • Logo, colors, or a format to match
Your part

Provide the facts and desired result

You approve the wording and confirm that the information is accurate.

Our part

Handle the document process

We gather, organize, draft, format, revise, quality-check, and deliver the finished file.
